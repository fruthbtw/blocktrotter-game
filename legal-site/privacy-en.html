Privacy Policy

Blocktrotter: Slide Puzzle · Effective date: 2026-10-21

This policy explains what information the mobile game Blocktrotter: Slide Puzzle (“the Game”) uses and why. The Game is developed by Yunus Emre Uludağ (“we”, “us”). If you have a question, contact us at ynsyutup@gmail.com.

1. No account, no personal details

The Game has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

2. Game progress stays on your device

Your progress — unlocked levels, stars, hints and settings (language, sound) — is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server. Deleting the Game deletes this data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner on the main menu, an interstitial ad after some completed levels, and an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch for an extra hint. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone/iPad only if you allow tracking — see section 4),
  • device and app information (device model, operating-system version, app version, language),
  • approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data such as crash information from the SDK.

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive data that identifies you personally.

4. Your choices

  • Tracking on iOS (App Tracking Transparency): the Game asks whether it may track you across apps. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, your advertising identifier is not shared and you still see ads, but they are less personalized. You can change this any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: we use Google's consent tool to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in the Game under Settings › Privacy Options.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

5. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live — for example under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) — you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests concern the data Google processes for ads; you can exercise your choices as described in section 4 and through Google's tools. You can also write to ynsyutup@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. We do not sell personal information.

7. Analytics

The Game does not use any analytics or tracking service of its own. If this changes, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the app store release notes.
